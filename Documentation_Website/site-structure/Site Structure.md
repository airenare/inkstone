Your vault's folder layout is your site's URL layout. No configuration required — InkStone derives everything from where files live and what frontmatter they contain. See [[URL Mapping]] for the full mapping table, [[Page Types]] for the available `type:` values, and [[Navigation]] for nav and breadcrumb behaviour.
