# The Accidental Existentialist

This post lives at the root of the vault, which means its URL has no prefix. It is categorized by the absence of category. It belongs to no section because it belongs to all of them — or perhaps to none. The difference is philosophical and, at this point, mostly academic.

## On Being Unfiled

Most posts know where they live. A blog post lives in `/blog`. A gallery post lives in `/gallery`. This post lives at `/test_post`, which is a fine address but says nothing about what you will find here.

This is, in fact, the entire point.

> [!note] About This Post
> This post exists to verify that root-level posts (files placed directly in the vault root, not in any subfolder) are accessible at `yoursite.com/slug` with no section prefix. It also exists because someone needed to write something creative at 11pm.

## A Short Taxonomy of Things Without Categories

- This post
- The miscellaneous drawer in every kitchen
- The "other" option in dropdown menus
- Emails that are neither spam nor important
- Most of what I have bookmarked "to read later"

## Conclusion

Being uncategorized is not the same as being lost. Sometimes a thing simply exists at the root level, accessible, functional, and entirely unbothered by the absence of a folder.

`/test_post`, home of the uncategorized. Yours sincerely.
